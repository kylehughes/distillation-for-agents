## Distinguish the inquiry

Distill's working standard is to reduce unnecessary burden while retaining what an identified use requires. Treat this as a practical synthesis. Do not attribute it to the whole historical tradition or claim that it proves what things ultimately are.

Distinguish a constitutive definition, material composition, causal mechanism, function, invariant, and action-relevant distinction. Aristotle's and Husserl's ambitions concerning essence exceed an adequate task-relative model. Conversely, a predictive model need not identify a unique mechanism. [R09] [R28]

Keep theoretical economy, ontological parsimony, brevity, familiarity, and ease of calculation separate. Philosophical accounts of why simplicity should guide inquiry remain disputed. Do not use a compact explanation as evidence of its own truth. [R52]

## Examine impressions and meanings

**Marcus Aurelius.** Meditations 3.11 examines a thing's plain description, constituents, duration, relation to the whole, and the virtue required in responding. Books 8.11–13 add multiple perspectives; 8.49 separates a report from an added judgment. Adapt this into a distinction between observations, interpretations, unknowns, and justified responses. Preserve ethical obligations and relevant context. Marcus's Stoic position concerning harm is stronger than this practical exercise in evidence. [R01] [R02]

Use the physical redescriptions of 6.13 to interrupt prestige and inherited valuation. Then restore arrangement, use, relationships, and experience before evaluating the whole. The reduction of music into separate elements in 11.2 serves detachment; it does not show that organization has no value. Treat this limitation as a check against selective debunking. In 4.24, fewer activities are qualified by the duties of a rational, social person. [R03] [R04] [R05]

**Epictetus.** Separate a choice from an outcome influenced by other causes. Use this to locate effective action without treating external events or collective action as irrelevant. The Enchiridion's ethical claims extend beyond this planning adaptation. [R06]

**Peirce and Feynman.** Ask what conceivable consequences give a concept its content, and explain a mechanism without relying on the label that names it. Peirce's practical consequences include hypothetical cases; they are not merely immediate usefulness. Feynman's naming exercise does not authorize replacing technical precision with a vague analogy. [R27] [R35]

**Orwell and Thoreau.** Make language answer to an examined meaning; preserve conditions and uncertainty when deleting words. Orwell's final rule resists mechanical obedience to brevity. Examine the purposes of commitments before optimizing them, as Thoreau's letter and Walden invite. Neither author supplies a universal preference for the fewest words or obligations. [R37] [R36] [R53]

## Test definitions and structure

**Plato.** Meno distinguishes examples from the account that makes them instances of a kind. Phaedrus pairs collection with division according to the subject's articulations. Adapt these into assembling instances, noninstances, and boundary cases, then testing necessity and sufficiency separately. A carving metaphor does not identify the correct boundaries automatically. [R07] [R08]

**Aristotle.** Separate embodiment from defining organization. An inventory of ingredients need not explain a whole. Distinguish material, formal, efficient, and final explanation; for artifacts and institutions, separate origin, intended purpose, and actual function. Do not silently extend purposeful design to every natural event. Seek the degree of precision appropriate to the subject and decision. [R09] [R10] [R11]

**Husserl.** Vary an example in imagination to challenge candidate invariants. His eidetic inquiry aims at necessity, rather than repeated empirical features. In practical work, use variation to generate hypotheses and counterexamples; inability to imagine an alternative does not prove impossibility. Seek actual alternatives or a suitable argument. [R28]

**Wittgenstein.** A shared word need not imply one feature common to all applications; overlapping similarities may sustain a category. Permit families or explicit subtypes when one abstraction becomes strained. Do not infer that exact definitions are always impossible. [R29]

## Investigate restraint and dependence

**Laozi.** Read the Daodejing as an ancient composite text traditionally attributed to Laozi. Chapters 48, 57, and 63 respectively suggest diminishing acquired interference, questioning proliferating controls, and attending to difficulty early. Ask whether an intervention helps reproduce the problem it addresses. Do not equate wuwei with laziness or general nonintervention. [R12] [R13] [R14]

**Zhuangzi.** Cook Ding follows articulations and spaces, develops skill through practice, and slows at difficult joints. Use persistent resistance as a reason to inspect the chosen division or sequence. Apparent ease does not imply absent discipline, and the resemblance to Plato's carving language does not establish a common metaphysics. [R15]

**Early Buddhist texts and Nagarjuna.** Vajira's chariot illustration examines designation dependent on assembled parts; the speaker is the nun Vajira. Kaccayanagotta examines conditional arising and cessation. Nagarjuna challenges intrinsic nature understood as independent and unmade while retaining conventional designation. Investigate whether relationships constitute the object before stripping them away. These liberation-oriented teachings are not simply early systems theory, and dependence does not imply causal ineffectiveness. [R16] [R17] [R18]

## Seek economical explanations

**Ockham and Newton.** Remove commitments whose explanatory work can be done without them. Preserve adequacy to the relevant phenomena. Newton also permits revision as new phenomena require qualification. Neither approach licenses selecting the easiest story regardless of evidence. The Ockham passage used here is translated within Sober's scholarly study; familiar razor formulations require attribution care. Newton's Rule IV was added in the 1726 third edition. [R19] [R20] [R23]

**Leibniz and Einstein.** Count independent commitments and the richness of consequences they support. Leibniz observes that arbitrary points can fit a sufficiently elaborate rule; merely producing a rule establishes little. Einstein relates theoretical economy to irreducible elements while retaining empirical adequacy. A simpler foundation may demand more difficult derivation. Treat connections to modern model selection as retrospective; Leibniz's argument has a theological setting. [R24] [R26]

**Rissanen and Grunwald.** Introductory two-part minimum description length counts both a model and the data described using it. Adapt this outside formal statistics as accounting for rules, parameters, exceptions, and conventions. A terse principle with many independent patches can increase total burden. Do not pretend a verbal assessment implements refined MDL or that coding choices are immaterial. [R45] [R46]

**Poincare.** Simple underlying rules can produce complicated appearances, while complicated processes can yield simple aggregate regularities. Identify the scale, precision, approximation, or limiting case behind a pattern. Do not infer that simplicity at one level describes the entire phenomenon. [R31]

## Analyze, reconstruct, and challenge

**Descartes and Polya.** Division must be followed by reconstruction and review. Descartes's four rules include disciplined assent, orderly movement through dependencies, and completeness. Polya adds related problems, specialization, generalization, working backward, and recombination. Record temporarily relaxed conditions and restore them when returning to the original task. A coherent derivation remains dependent on its premises. [R21] [R34]

**Dewey.** Turn diffuse difficulty into a question, develop possible solutions, derive consequences, and investigate them. Avoid defining the problem as the absence of a favored solution. Observation already involves interpretation; separating observations and inferences is a useful discipline, not access to wholly unclassified data. [R33]

**Bacon and James.** Actively seek cases a satisfying account handles badly. Bacon warns about projected order and favoring confirming instances. James describes both the attraction of unification and the desire to distinguish particulars. Treat intellectual relief as a prompt to investigate what the account omitted. Do not make every anomaly an automatic refutation without examining measurement and auxiliary assumptions. [R22] [R30]

**Kant and Whitehead.** Pair unification with specification: ask where cases still differ. Kant's regulative unity guides investigation without directly establishing the object's ultimate constitution. Whitehead questions confidence in an age's final vocabulary. Use scope and revision conditions to make intellectual distrust productive rather than paralyzing. [R25] [R32]

## Preserve interactions and response capacity

**Weaver and Simon.** Diagnose the interaction structure before choosing an analysis. Organized complexity may disappear when variables are isolated or averaged. Simon explains useful near decomposability through differences in interaction strength and timescale. Inspect actual cross-boundary effects; convenient organizational partitions do not establish independence. Do not treat Weaver's historical categories as limits on contemporary statistical methods. [R38] [R39]

**Dijkstra, Parnas, and Brooks.** Examine correctness, desirability, and cost separately, then reconcile their tradeoffs. Place boundaries around changeable decisions and expose the commitments others need. Distinguish conceptual demands of the selected task from burdens of its implementation. Challenge inherited requirements where authorized; calling one essential does not make it immutable. These are design adaptations, not claims that every subject behaves like software. Brooks's historical productivity forecasts are not used as current predictions. [R40] [R41] [R42]

**Ashby.** Specify disturbances, available responses, information, and protected outcomes before applying requisite variety. Preserve or recover distinctions needed for competent response. Do not summarize the law as a requirement that every controller reproduce all environmental complexity. [R43]

**Shannon.** Specify permissible loss before compressing. Coding with a fidelity criterion makes distortion part of the problem definition. Use this as an analogy for choosing what a representation may omit; the mathematics does not choose human values or settle which errors are acceptable. [R44]

## Audit omissions and interventions

**Box.** Direct attention to consequential inadequacies and iterate between models and observations. A model's omissions are judged against its use, but a useful model is not thereby a uniquely correct causal account. Avoid treating all incomplete models as equally good. [R47]

**Meadows.** Examine accumulations, flows, delays, feedback, information, rules, and goals when a problem recurs. Observe behavior over time and expose the mental model to criticism. Treat the proposed ordering of intervention points as tentative, not a recipe guaranteeing that a higher-listed intervention will work. [R48] [R49]

**Ostrom.** Use a structured diagnostic framework while allowing context to change the remedy. This supplies a model for domain-independent questions with domain-dependent answers. Do not turn it into a universal instruction to decentralize. [R50]

**Scott.** Ask whose work becomes easier and whose knowledge becomes invisible. Administrative legibility can omit local relationships and transfer burdens. Distinguish making a map from forcing the world to match it. Where uncertainty remains, favor appropriately limited, reversible changes and channels through which omitted knowledge can correct the plan. [R51]

## Keep the adaptation honest

Use historical sources to explain a method's origin or limits; use task-specific evidence to establish that it works here. Philosophical authority does not validate an implementation, intervention, or recommendation.

Keep preservation of meaning, empirical adequacy, causal identification, operational resilience, and expressive quality distinct. Explanatory redundancy may be removable while operational redundancy provides a needed failure response. No single simplicity score combines these dimensions without substantive judgments.

Verify wording before presenting quotations. Do not attribute the popular simplicity slogan to Einstein's 1933 lecture, the internet's four-step learning routine wholesale to Feynman, or Vajira's chariot speech to the Buddha. Prefer accurate paraphrase with a passage locator when exact wording is unnecessary. [R26] [R35] [R16]
